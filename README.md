### 📂 Featured Portfolio Projects

#### 1. [Geospatial Analysis of Urban Heat Island Vulnerability in Phoenix, AZ](https://github.com/ProgressMomoh/Geospatial-Analysis-of-Urban-Heat-Island-UHI-Vulnerability-in-Phoenix-AZ)
- **Scope:** Citywide urban heat and climate vulnerability assessment.
- **Overview:** A spatial heat analysis across 15 Phoenix urban villages using Landsat 8 multispectral imagery and Collection 2 Level-2 surface temperature data. Calculated NDVI and NDBI indices to map vegetation and concrete ratios, identifying a 4.7°C surface temperature gap between Central City (62.3°C) and North Gateway (57.6°C) to highlight priority zones for urban shade infrastructure and cool pavements.
- **Key Skills:** Landsat 8 satellite processing, spectral indexing (NDVI/NDBI), zonal statistics, temperature data calibration, QGIS dual-map cartography.

#### 2. [WRPC Operational HSE and Containment Strategy](https://github.com/ProgressMomoh/WRPC-HSE-Spatial-Assessment-Project)
- **Scope:** Micro scale facility analysis and predeployment planning.
- **Overview:** A site specific operational risk assessment for a 40 hectare industrial facility. I manually digitized 10 critical storage assets and mapped 50m hazard buffers to visualize cascade risks. I also engineered spatial containment strategies, including a Cold Zone safety area and a runoff barrier to protect the nearby Ekpan River from potential contamination.
- **Key Skills:** Primary data generation, vertex digitization, CRS projection management.

#### 3. [Düsseldorf Spatial Risk Assessment](https://github.com/ProgressMomoh/Dusseldorf-geospatial-risk-assessment)
- **Scope:** Macro level urban and ecological risk modeling.
- **Overview:** A regional spatial analysis mapping the intersection between industrial hazard zones and vulnerable public receptors across Düsseldorf, Germany. I sourced authoritative open source vector data and cleaned the datasets to filter out irrelevant noise before mapping the exact points where industrial activity threatened ecological green spaces.
- **Key Skills:** SQL data cleaning, spatial intersections, large dataset management.
